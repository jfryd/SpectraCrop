# SpectraCrop Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 31, 2026

## Introduction

SpectraCrop ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Personal Information
We collect the following personal information when you create an account:
- **Username** - Used to identify your account
- **Password** - Securely hashed and stored

### 2. Device Information
To provide core functionality, we automatically collect:
- **Device Identifier** - A unique identifier for your device, used to associate readings with your device and prevent abuse
- **Device Model** - Used for compatibility and debugging
- **Operating System Version** - Used to ensure App compatibility

### 3. Location Information
With your explicit consent, we collect:
- **Precise Location Data** (latitude, longitude, altitude) - Attached to your readings to provide geospatial analysis
- **Location is optional** - You can create readings without location data

### 4. Usage Data
We collect information about your use of the App:
- **Reading Data** - Spectral measurements, timestamps, and metadata
- **Sync Status** - Information about data synchronization with our servers
- **App Usage Statistics** - Feature usage patterns to improve the App

### 5. Bluetooth Data
When you connect to a SpectraCrop hardware device:
- **Device Name** - The name of your Bluetooth device
- **Device Identifier** - Unique Bluetooth identifier
- **Signal Strength** - Used to establish and maintain connection

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

### App Functionality
- To enable you to create, view, and manage spectral readings
- To connect to SpectraCrop hardware devices via Bluetooth
- To display readings on maps and charts
- To synchronize data across your devices

### Account Management
- To create and manage your user account
- To authenticate your access to the App
- To maintain your session state

### Improvements
- To analyze usage patterns and improve App performance
- To develop new features based on user needs
- To debug and fix issues

### Communications
- To send you service-related notifications (e.g., sync status)
- To respond to your support requests

## How We Share Your Information

### Third-Party Service Providers
We may share your information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf:
- **Hosting Services** - Our backend servers for data storage and processing
- **Analytics Services** - To help us understand App usage (aggregated, anonymous data only)

### Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

### Business Transfers
In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of the business transaction.

**We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.**

## Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide the App services:
- **User Account Data** - Retained for the lifetime of your account plus 90 days after deletion
- **Reading Data** - Retained for the lifetime of your account plus 90 days after deletion
- **Device Identifiers** - Retained for session management and App functionality
- **Anonymous Usage Data** - May be retained indefinitely in aggregated form

You may request deletion of your data at any time by:
1. Deleting your account within the App (Settings > Delete Account)
2. Contacting us at privacy@spectracrop.com

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:
- **Encryption** - Data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
- **Secure Storage** - Sensitive data is stored securely on your device
- **Access Controls** - Access to your data is restricted to authorized personnel only
- **Regular Audits** - We conduct regular security reviews of our systems

**Note:** No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

### Right to Access
You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you.

### Right to Correction
You have the right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.

### Right to Deletion
You have the right to request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions (e.g., legal requirements).

### Right to Data Portability
You have the right to request a copy of your data in a portable format.

### Right to Opt-Out
You have the right to opt-out of certain data processing activities.

## Bluetooth Permissions

The App requires Bluetooth access to:
- Discover and connect to SpectraCrop hardware devices
- Transmit and receive spectral measurement data
- Maintain connection for real-time readings

You can revoke Bluetooth permissions at any time in your device settings.

## Location Permissions

The App requests location permissions to:
- Attach geospatial coordinates to your readings
- Display readings on maps
- Provide location-based features

Location data is **only collected when you explicitly create a reading with location enabled**. You can:
- Grant location access only while using the App
- Deny location access entirely
- Revoke location permissions at any time in device settings

## Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will take steps to delete it.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the updated Privacy Policy in the App and updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this document.

Your continued use of the App after we post changes constitutes your acceptance of those changes.
